training/benchmark.py: false wakes per hour over three negative sets, each
played as one continuous stream with a 2 s refractory period:
  LibriSpeech test-clean   5.4 h of read speech, close microphone
  AMI SDM test             8.9 h of meetings, one distant microphone
  People's Speech dirty    9.8 h of noisy found audio (2 of 11 test shards)
Catches, each clip padded with a second of silence either side:
  Real voice   takes/heldout: 16 takes of "computah" and "hey computah" by
               one speaker of the target accent, recorded on a desk
               microphone and never trained on (their other 20 are). Wakes
               on takes/not: 23 takes of "computer", "compute",
               "commuter", "Jupiter", "potato", other speech and silence.
  Approved     240 clips by the five Piper voices training never uses
  endings      (2 British, 3 American), in the four endings that speaker
               approved: tah, dah (soft t), duh and taw; bare and "hey",
               3 speeds, 2 noise levels, unscreened (data/heldout_end).
  In noise     those 240 through a real room impulse response and into AMI
               or People's Speech audio at 10, 5 and 0 dB; 720 clips
               (data/hard_end, training/openwakeword/hardtest.sh).
  Old ending   v3 and v4's sets: 106 clips by the same five voices in the
               long "ah" ending ("ta-da"), and 564 in noise (data/hard).

Training's negatives include other AMI meetings and other LibriSpeech
speakers, so People's Speech is the set furthest from training.
