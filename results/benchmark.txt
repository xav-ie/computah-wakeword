training/benchmark.py: false wakes per hour over three negative sets, each
played as one continuous stream with a 2 s refractory period:
  LibriSpeech test-clean   5.4 h of read speech, close microphone
  AMI SDM test             8.9 h of meetings, one distant microphone
  People's Speech dirty    9.8 h of noisy found audio (2 of 11 test shards)
Catches: 106 held-out clips of "computah" and "hey computah" from five Piper
voices training never uses (2 British, 3 American), made from phonemes and
screened like the training clips (training/openwakeword).
Harder test (training/openwakeword/hardtest.sh): those 106 clips and 82 from
four held-out Kokoro voices, each through a real room impulse response and
into AMI or People's Speech audio at 10, 5 and 0 dB; 564 clips.

Training's negatives include other AMI meetings and other LibriSpeech
speakers, so People's Speech is the set furthest from training.
